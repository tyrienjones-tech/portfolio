The result

I self-host this on hardware I control. It scores an SSL Labs A+, behind locked-down security headers and a strict content policy. Nothing a visitor does on here is sent to anyone but this site.

Fast, private, and boring on purpose. Nothing fragile, nothing to break, nothing phoning home.
