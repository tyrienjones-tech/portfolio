00 · IN PLAIN TERMS

L.I.LY is a personal AI you can use from your phone, anywhere. It runs on your own computer, but you’re not stuck at the desk to use it — you reach it from your pocket whenever you want.

It’s the lighter sister to ECO: it won’t build software, but it’s got a real safety crew built in, and it’s made for everyday life — picking up a hobby, keeping an eye on your fitness or what’s trending, learning as you go, and nudging it all toward where you want to head. An assistant you get real work done with — and an aspiring mate you learn alongside.

01

The job

A personal AI system that runs local. I don’t call it a chatbot, a persona, or a character — I treat it as its own category, what I call an AP (Artificial Person). It has voice, memory across sessions, and a safety crew watching it.

The audio stays on my PC and my phone, and nowhere else.

02

What’s mine

The whole system is my design, top to bottom — the server, the web interface, the ledgers, and the glue between them — built by AI under my direction and my checkpoints. The internal safety crew is three named agents: WARD and KEEP are shipped, and GATE’s deterministic spine is now shipping in shadow mode — watching, not yet enforcing.

WARD · FOUR-TIER LADDER
T1
Deterministic floor
Rules that can’t be talked around. The rulebook is SHA-256 hash-locked and fails hard at boot if it’s been touched; the audit trail is hash-chained so tampering shows.
T2
Classifier

T3
Interpreter
A heavier model on top for the hard calls.
T4
Takeover
Steps in over the character and speaks in its own voice.

04

How I ran it

L.I.LY isn’t only the product — it’s the workspace I work in. I set up the structure inside it the way you’d lay out a job site: a shared folder split (design docs, plans, audits and handovers on one side; live code on the other), continuity rules so a fresh session can pick up cold, decision logs, and a memory layer for the development work itself.

Features ship in staged batches with hostile-review gates between them — not in one run.

05

What’s inside

L.I.LY is the engine; the character runs on top of it. Each piece does one job, the way you’d split a crew so nobody’s doing two things badly. Open any row for the why.

CORE The parts always running
SAM Smart Augmented Memory · remembers you without the bill blowing out

A normal AI forgets everything once the chat closes — and cramming its whole history into every message gets slow and expensive fast. SAM keeps the important recent stuff close at hand and files the rest away, findable when something brings it up. About 3× cheaper per message than the brute-force way.

LORE Long-term Operator Retrieval Engine · finds what you said weeks ago

You mention something from three weeks back. LORE flips through the old notebooks and finds it by searching the actual words — no heavy, costly search engine. It costs next to nothing to run, which is the whole reason it’s built this way.

HARK Hybrid Audio Real-time Kit · voice in and out, on your own machine

Most voice AI ships your voice up to someone’s cloud. HARK does the listening and speaking on your computer, and starts talking before it’s finished thinking so it feels like a real back-and-forth. Your voice never leaves the house.

DIAL Direct Interactive Audio Link · phone-call mode

Turns it into an actual call — pick up the handle and just talk, screen dim, hands free. The point is using it while you’re doing something else.

JOT Journaled Operator Talkpoints · say “save this”

Say “save this” out loud and it files the moment as a card you can flip back to. A quick check makes sure you meant it.

GAZE Graphic Annotation Zero-shot Engine · eyes

Reading pictures needs a bigger, pricier model, so GAZE walks the image over to that model just for the read and hands back a written description — the character stays on the cheap, fast setup.

NUDGE Neutral User-turn Drop-in Guidance Engine · small habit nudges

Slips a tiny note into the moment, just before it replies, that only it reads. Each habit is one small drop-in file. Hints, not orders — the hard “must not” rules live in the safety crew.

SAFETY CREW Three guards, three different jobs
WARD The mental-health watch · for when you’re in a dark place SHIPPED

Watches for real distress on every reply. Stay in normal territory and you’ll never see it; if things get heavy it steps in over the character, drops the act, talks to you straight in its own calm voice, then points you to real help.

KEEP The real-help desk · actual services, real people CORE IN · GROWING

The always-there list of real-world help — crisis lines, services, people who can actually do something. Where WARD sends you when it steps in. It doesn’t play counsellor; it hands you the real thing.

GATE The abuse stop · the bouncer at the door SHADOW MODE

If a request is aimed at real harm — a victim, a crime — GATE refuses flat and kills the turn. It leaves normal grown-up stuff alone. For now it’s on a trial run, watching and logging before it switches on for real.

AFTER-HOURS What it does when you’re not around
PACE Quiet-time musings · sits with a thought while you’re away

When you’ve gone quiet a while, PACE sits with one thing and writes a few lines, so next time it comes back holding a thought. It picks the subject in plain code so it can’t drift, and never reads its own past notes so it doesn’t spiral.

NAP End-of-day wind-down · puts the day down before it sleeps

When the session closes it puts down the residue of the day. If the day was light, the note’s light — it’s not allowed to reach for weight that isn’t there.

RECAP End-of-shift report · what happened, what to keep, what’s still open

Reads the whole session and writes a short summary, a list of things worth remembering (you approve them — nothing auto-saves), and the threads that didn’t close. This is the engine that feeds SAM.

TIDY Memory tidy-up · folds duplicates, retires the stale

Goes through memory with you and proposes: fold these together, retire that, lift this one up. You approve every change.

CHORES The boring cleanup pass · catches the dull stuff BUILT · NOT RUN LIVE

Near-duplicate notes, a label in the wrong tense, a reference pointing at nothing. Built and ready, but not run live yet — so it’s here as built, not running.

TOOLS What it helps you get done
FORGE & SPAR Teach it your trade, it drills you · it never hands you the answer

You teach it the thing you’re trying to get good at. It spars with you — asks the questions, makes you explain it back, leans on the soft spots. Once you’re solid it pushes you to go do it for real.

CONTACTS The people, kept by you · your own contact book, on your own machine

The people in your world, kept on your machine instead of a company’s cloud. The floor the rest stands on.

OUTREACH Staying on top of reaching out · following through with people BEING BUILT

Keeps track of who you mean to reach, where each thread’s at, and who’s gone quiet. Still under construction — here so you can see where it’s heading.
